IW modifications: Fabric 1.20.1 Mojang API/config/registry integration; fixed
serialized simple dust type (constant color/scale); reuse IW sand layers;
loaded/dry/supported deposition and no-write cap fixes; temporary IW-specific
modifier reconciliation; Scorchful coexistence; vanilla fog safeguards,
particle setting/budget limits and world-aware sound scheduling.
